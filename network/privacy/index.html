Privacy Policy

How we handle your information — POPIA-aligned

This policy explains what personal information Smoke Signals Network collects when you apply for or hold membership, and how it's used, in line with South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA).

1. What we collect

  • Name and surname
  • WhatsApp number
  • Area / general location
  • Age confirmation (18+)
  • Anything you choose to tell us in the application (referral, notes)
  • Membership and payment status (active, lapsed, cancelled) once you're a member

2. Why we collect it

To assess and process your membership application, communicate with you about your membership, run recurring billing for the monthly membership fee, and organise club events. We don't collect more than we need for these purposes.

3. Where it's stored

Application and membership data is stored in a private Google Sheet accessible only to the Club's administrators, via Google Apps Script. Membership fees are collected manually (e.g. bank transfer or cash) — we do not use an automated payment processor, and no card details are collected or stored by us.

4. Who we share it with

We don't sell or share your information with third parties for marketing. It's shared only with the service providers needed to operate the Club (e.g. Google, for storage), and only to the extent needed for those services to work.

5. How long we keep it

We keep member data for as long as your membership is active, plus a reasonable period after cancellation for record-keeping. Declined or abandoned applications are retained only as long as needed to review them.

6. Your rights

Under POPIA, you have the right to:

  • Ask what personal information we hold about you
  • Ask us to correct inaccurate information
  • Ask us to delete your information (subject to any legal obligation to retain it)
  • Object to how your information is processed
  • Lodge a complaint with South Africa's Information Regulator if you believe your rights have been infringed

To exercise any of these rights, contact us via WhatsApp at +27 83 237 4344 or email network@s-signals.com.

7. Cookies & local storage

The site uses your browser's local storage only to prevent rapid duplicate form submissions. No tracking or advertising cookies are used.
